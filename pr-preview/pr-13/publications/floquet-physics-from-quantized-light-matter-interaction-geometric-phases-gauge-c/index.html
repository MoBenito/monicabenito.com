Floquet physics from quantized light-matter interaction: geometric phases, gauge consistency, and entanglement

Beatriz Pérez-González, Sigmund Kohler, Mónica Benito

Preprint (2026)

arXivBibTeXRIS Cited by 3

How Floquet physics of classically driven systems emerges from fully quantized light-matter interaction.

Floquet engineering and cavity quantum electrodynamics describe light-matter interaction in complementary ways, and their exact connection beyond weak coupling is subtle. Representing the cavity field in a large-photon-number phase basis, we show that the time-dependent Schrödinger equation of a classically driven system follows directly from the fully quantized problem. This gives a one-to-one correspondence between quasienergies and quantum energies, a quantum interpretation of the Floquet mean energy and Anandan phase, and, since gauge invariance is preserved exactly, holds beyond the small-coupling regime. Light-matter entanglement is encoded in the correlations of the Floquet mode.

Research area: Light–matter interaction
