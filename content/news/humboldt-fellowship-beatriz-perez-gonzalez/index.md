Congratulations to Beatriz Pérez González, who has won a Humboldt Research Fellowship from the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation! Beatriz joined our group in 2024, and since March 2026 she continues her research here as a Humboldt fellow.

She studies what happens when light and matter interact strongly in semiconductor spin qubits, and how microwave photons could link qubits that sit far apart on a chip. Her theoretical models look for realistic devices in which this regime can be reached and put to use.

The university portrays her and her project: [Humboldt Research Fellow researches quantum technology](https://www.uni-augsburg.de/en/campusleben/neuigkeiten/2026/06/10/humboldt-research-fellow-researches-quantum-technology/).
