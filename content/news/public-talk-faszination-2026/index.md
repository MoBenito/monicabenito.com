I gave a public talk in the series [Faszination Mathematik und Physik](https://www.uni-a.de/de/fakultaet/mntf/infos/veranstaltungen/faszination/2026/) of the University of Augsburg, in the Zeughaus Augsburg: *Wenn Spins rechnen lernen – Auf dem Weg zu skalierbaren Quantencomputern* (when spins learn to compute: on the way to scalable quantum computers).

Quantum computers promise computing power beyond today's computers, but for that single qubits have to get better and millions of them have to work together. Since all classical computers are built on semiconductor chips, it makes sense to build quantum computers from semiconductors too, with qubits made from the spin of single electrons. The talk followed these spins as they learn to compute, one trick at a time.
