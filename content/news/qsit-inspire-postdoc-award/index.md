I was awarded the INSPIRE award for postdocs launched by the National Center of Competence in Research *Quantum Science and Technology*.

The award supports excellent women for a postdoc project in a group of the QSIT network; mine is with Jelena Klinovaja and Daniel Loss in Basel. See the [list of awardees](https://web.archive.org/web/20220927080236/https://nccr-qsit.ethz.ch/equal-opportunity/qsit-inspire-postdoc-award/awardess.html) (archived copy; the NCCR QSIT has ended and its website is offline).
