Our group is now part of [GeMOS](https://gemos.physics.sk/index.html), a QuantERA project funded through the DFG in Germany, together with teams in Bratislava (coordinator Peter Stano), Regensburg, Basel and ETH Zurich.

The project studies holes in germanium metal-oxide-semiconductor nanostructures, a material platform that has not been used for spin qubits before and fits well with industrial chip manufacturing. Bringing together material growth, quantum theory and measurement, the goal is scalable germanium MOSFET devices that host competitive spin qubits. We contribute the theory.
