I gave one of the opening keynotes at the 5th [Virtual Engine Conference](https://www.dlr.de/en/sg/latest/news/5th-virtual-engine-conference-2026-1) of the DLR Institute of Test and Simulation for Gas Turbines in Augsburg: *Modelling and simulation of scalable quantum computers*.

Over 70 participants from science and industry, from gas turbines to AI-driven engineering, met to discuss the latest digital developments. A nice chance to show how quantum computers are designed on the computer before they are built, and what they might one day compute for engineering.
