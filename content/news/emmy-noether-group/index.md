The German Research Foundation (DFG) will fund my Emmy Noether junior research group *[Spin-based Floquet quantum information processor](https://gepris.dfg.de/project/496173695/2?lang=en)*.

Spin qubits in semiconductor quantum dots are a promising route to large quantum computers, but they still suffer from charge noise and only interact with their nearest neighbours. The idea of the project is to put time to work: qubits driven periodically in time (Floquet engineering) can be placed where noise hardly reaches them, switched on and off for long-distance couplings, used to probe the noise itself, and even organised into time crystals that keep their order while computing.
