From April 2023 I will be a professor at the University of Augsburg and I am inviting motivated scientists to join our group to work on Quantum information theory based on solid state spin qubits.
We have two PhD positions and two postdoc positions.

[PhD positions](https://www.quantiki.org/position/2-phd-positions-theoretical-quantum-computing-modeling-and-simulation): Theoretical modeling and simulation of spin qubits in quantum dots. Send your CV, letter of motivation, and the name and contact data from a possible reference by [email](mailto:monica.benito@uni-a.de). Feel free to contact me for further information!

[Postdoc positions](https://www.quantiki.org/position/2-postdoc-positions-theoretical-quantum-computing): One position is to investigate many-body aspects of driven quantum devices and the other one for quantum simulation of quantum-dots systems. Research experience on quantum technologies, periodically driven systems, quantum many-body systems, quantum algorithms and/or quantum simulation is required. Send your CV, letter of motivation, and the name and contact data from two possible references by [email](mailto:monica.benito@uni-a.de). Feel free to contact me for further information!
