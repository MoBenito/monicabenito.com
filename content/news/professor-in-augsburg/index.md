Since April 2023 I am professor for Quantum Information and Quantum Computing at the University of Augsburg, where I had already been an interim professor in 2021. With the Emmy Noether programme of the German Research Foundation (DFG) I am building up a junior research group here.

The university introduced its newly appointed professors, me among them: [Neu nach Augsburg berufen](https://www.uni-augsburg.de/de/campusleben/neuigkeiten/2023/06/02/neu-an-die-universitaet-berufen/) (in German).
