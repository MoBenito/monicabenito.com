Our group is part of [QuKiT](https://web.archive.org/web/20260715101906/https://qukit.eu/member/monica-benito/), a new project funded by the European Innovation Council (EIC) Pathfinder Challenge, which started with a kickoff meeting in Delft. It brings together several research disciplines, industry and EU platforms around a new kind of qubit, the Kitmon. The goal is a qubit that is immune to local sources of noise and still easy to control, so that useful quantum computing would need a few thousand qubits rather than a few million.

Building on our experience with spin qubits in silicon and germanium quantum dots, we lead the design and optimisation of gate schemes to control and manipulate the Kitmon qubit.
