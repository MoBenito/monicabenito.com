A charge qubit on solid neon with fast coherent control, coupled to a magnetic-field-compatible resonator.

Electrons on solid neon are a promising qubit platform with potentially long coherence times. Toward spin qubits, we couple a single electron on solid neon to a superconducting NbTiN nanowire resonator that tolerates magnetic fields. We realize a charge qubit with microwave readout and coherent control at Rabi frequencies up to 76 MHz, an order of magnitude faster than before, and observe a qubit frequency shift under strong driving. We also determine the electron's position from its coupling to different electrodes; our estimates indicate that spin-qubit demonstrations remain feasible.
