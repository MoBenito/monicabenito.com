Superconducting NbTiN nanowire resonators keep their high quality on solid neon, a step toward spin qubits with electrons on neon.

Electrons floating on solid neon have long charge coherence times, which makes them attractive for hybrid quantum systems. We show that NbTiN nanowire resonators keep quality factors around 10⁵ after solid neon is deposited and electrons are loaded onto its surface. Building on this, we analyze micromagnet designs and coupling strategies for spin-photon interaction, and find that at the charge sweet spot, single-qubit gate fidelities above 99.99% and two-qubit gate fidelities above 99.9% are within reach with natural neon.
