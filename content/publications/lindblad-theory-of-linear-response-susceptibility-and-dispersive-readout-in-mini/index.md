A Lindblad-based linear response theory for readout of hybrid superconductor-semiconductor quantum dots.

Hybrid superconductor-semiconductor quantum dots need a complete theory of how they respond to probes and readout, including dissipation from their environment. We present a Lindblad-based linear response formalism that captures their multi-level nature and the non-unitary effects of finite-frequency response, including the Sisyphus and Hermes susceptibilities that standard Kubo theory misses. Applied to quantum-dot Kitaev chains, candidates for Majorana-based parity qubits, it tests the usual curvature-based approximation for parity and qubit readout and shows that Hermes terms compensate decoherence in dispersive readout.
