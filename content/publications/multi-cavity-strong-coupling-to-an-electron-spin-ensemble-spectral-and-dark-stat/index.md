Several superconducting cavities strongly coupled to one spin ensemble, with signatures of dark states.

Spin ensembles are candidates for quantum memories and quantum-enhanced sensing. We couple several superconducting microwave cavities to one spin ensemble and observe signatures of strong coupling and, thanks to the multi-mode setup, of dark states. Dark states could extend memory times and protect stored non-classical states, since their coupling to the circuit environment is suppressed. An input-output model reproduces the spectra and gives the characteristic coupling strengths.
