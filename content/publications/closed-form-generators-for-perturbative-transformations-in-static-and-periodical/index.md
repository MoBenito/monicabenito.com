A closed-form expression for the generator of perturbative transformations, for static and periodically driven systems.

We introduce a unified framework for perturbative transformations that overcomes limitations of existing methods. Its central result is a closed-form operator expression for the generator of a broad class of transformations in systems of finite-dimensional and bosonic subspaces. We extend it to periodically driven systems, with a solution valid at low, intermediate and high frequencies as long as the eliminated transitions stay detuned. We demonstrate it by deriving effective dispersive interactions in anharmonic and periodically driven light-matter systems.
