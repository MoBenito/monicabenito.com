A way to read out spins electrically even when spin blockade is lifted.

Spin blockade is the most scalable mechanism for electrical spin readout, but strong spin-orbit coupling or low-lying excited states can lift it. We present a readout method based on how the polarizability of the two-spin system depends on energy detuning, which turns the lifting of spin blockade into a resource. We demonstrate it with a quantum dot and a boron acceptor in a silicon transistor, read out different spin states under both lifting mechanisms, and measure detuning-dependent spin relaxation times of 0.1 to 8 µs.
