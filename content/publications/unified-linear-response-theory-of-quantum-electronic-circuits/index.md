One theory for the electrical response of quantum devices at any frequency, including relaxation and dephasing.

So far, the finite-frequency response of multi-level quantum systems has been described either by input-output theory, which works at any frequency but neglects dynamic losses, or by semiclassical theory, which captures dissipation but is only accurate at low frequencies. Our theory unifies both: any multi-level system is described by a universal small-signal equivalent circuit, a resonant RLC circuit whose topology depends only on the number of energy levels. We apply it to a double-quantum-dot charge qubit and to a Majorana qubit, continuously covering the adiabatic to resonant and coherent to incoherent regimes, and suggest realistic experiments for improved readout. The model will help design hybrid quantum-classical circuits and simulate qubit control and readout.
