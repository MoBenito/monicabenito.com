A spatially resolved model of flopping-mode spin qubits that goes beyond the usual two-site description.

We model the full spatial structure of the double-well confinement and of the magnetic-field gradient. For single-qubit control by electric dipole spin resonance, we find a tradeoff between fast driving and clean single-mode Rabi oscillations, and show that the standard two-site approximation can overestimate the Rabi frequency by up to about 20%. For two capacitively coupled flopping-mode qubits, we derive the exchange interaction and show how confinement, field gradient and Coulomb interaction together set its strength. The framework maps device properties directly to qubit parameters and quality metrics.
