A protocol to measure the quantum geometric tensor in tunnel and molecular junctions.

The quantum geometric tensor captures both the geometry and the topology of quantum states. It has been measured in several platforms, but not yet in electronic transport systems such as tunnel or molecular junctions. We propose a protocol based on weak, resonant parameter modulations and show theoretically that the local quantum geometric tensor can be read directly from changes in the tunnel conductance, giving access to geometric and topological features in a broad class of transport-based systems.
