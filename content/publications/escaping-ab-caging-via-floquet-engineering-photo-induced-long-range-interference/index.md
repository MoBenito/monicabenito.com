Periodic driving lets particles escape Aharonov-Bohm caging in a flat-band diamond chain.

Flat-band lattices with compact localized states react sensitively to external modulation. We show that high-frequency driving turns the all-flat-band diamond chain into one with two tunable quasi-flat bands and a flat band pinned at zero energy. Drive-induced tunneling redefines the interference conditions and opens a controllable route out of Aharonov-Bohm caging: the chain effectively becomes a dimerized lattice with charge oscillations between its ends, which can be used to create experimentally accessible two-particle entanglement.
