Hi! I’m Mónica Benito, a theoretical quantum physicist and professor at the University of Augsburg (Germany), where I lead the group [Quantum Information and Quantum Computing](https://www.uni-augsburg.de/en/fakultaet/mntf/physik/groups/qcode/).

My research is on [quantum information in solid-state systems](/research/spin-quantum-computing/), [Floquet engineering](/research/open-systems-dynamics/), [topological matter](/research/topological-matter/), [quantum transport](/research/open-systems-dynamics/) and quantum simulation.

Current topics in my group:

- Spin-based Floquet quantum information processing
- Quantum computing with spins in quantum dots
- Quantum simulation of condensed-matter systems
- Hybrid semiconductor–superconductor devices
