No quantum device is truly isolated: it exchanges energy and particles with its surroundings. This is usually seen as a nuisance that destroys quantum coherence, but it can also be a resource.

Engineered dissipation can create and stabilise entanglement instead of destroying it, and the electrons flowing through a device carry information about it in their current and its noise. I develop the methods for such systems, from master equations and the full counting statistics of transport to linear response theory for the readout of quantum devices.
