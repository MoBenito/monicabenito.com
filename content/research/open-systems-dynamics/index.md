No quantum device is truly isolated: it exchanges energy and particles with its surroundings, and it is controlled by fields that change in time. Both are usually seen as a nuisance. Both can also be a resource.

Periodic driving (Floquet engineering) can move a qubit to where noise hardly reaches it, or create effective interactions that a static system cannot have. Engineered dissipation can create and stabilise entanglement instead of destroying it. I develop the methods for such systems, from master equations and the full counting statistics of transport to linear response theory and effective Hamiltonians for driven systems, the latter also as the open-source tool [SymPT](/publications/sympt-a-comprehensive-tool-for-automating-effective-hamiltonian-derivations/).
