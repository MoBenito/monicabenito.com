## What this website collects

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other servers (no external fonts, videos or social media buttons).

When you open a page, the web server automatically records technical access data: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and the browser identification your browser sends. This is needed to deliver the website and to detect and block attacks. These logs are deleted after 14 days and are not combined with other data. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in running the website securely).

## Email

If you send me an email, I use your address and message only to reply to you.
